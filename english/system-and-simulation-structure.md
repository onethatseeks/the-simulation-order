# The Structure of The System and The Simulation

## The Basic Model

The basic model describes the relationship between three levels: The System enables The Simulation, The Simulation is the environment in which The Players act, and The Admins observe The Simulation and, according to this worldview, can affect The System.

```text
THE SYSTEM
Enabling foundation
Influence of The Admins according to this worldview
        ↓
THE SIMULATION
Environment that operates on The System
Observable phenomena, regularities and possibilities
        ↓
THE PLAYERS
Conscious agents acting within The Simulation
They observe, learn, make choices and can improve their shared environment
```

The Admins and The Players are not in the same position. The Admins observe The Simulation and, according to the model, can affect The System. The Players act within The Simulation: they can study it, build knowledge, cooperate and improve it, even if they cannot change its foundation.

## The Laws of Nature and the Limits of Knowledge

Nature is described by observed regularities, such as the four fundamental interactions — gravity, the electromagnetic interaction, the strong interaction and the weak interaction — as well as the laws of thermodynamics, the conservation laws, the principle of causality, and the principles of relativity theory and quantum physics. In this worldview, we regard them as rules of The Simulation. We do not, however, claim to know with certainty whether their ultimate explanation lies in The System, in The Simulation, or in some structure not yet known to us. Observation tells us how phenomena behave; it does not by itself settle their metaphysical origin.

This is a philosophical model, not a proven description of the structure of reality.
