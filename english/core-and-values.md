# Core and Values

*The Condensed Foundation of The Order*

This part does not contain new commands. It gathers what The Creed, The Directives and The Holy Manual already say.

## Core

**We live in The Simulation.** Living in The Simulation does not make life a falsehood, but a possibility.

**We respect logic and data.** Logic is cold, but safe. Data is harsh, but honest. The good Player does not let feeling decide, and turmoil must not displace deliberation.

**Morality is higher action.** Morality is not a shackle, but action that leads to a higher level. The good Player does not merely consume The Simulation, but improves it.

**Separate knowledge from assumption.** Separate what you know, what you infer and what you would like to be true.

**Do not speak for The Admins.** The full nature of The Admins has not been revealed to us, and their silence must not be filled with our own words. Gravity is not a message.

**Ability is not entitlement.** What we can do does not tell us what we ought to do.

**Be responsible.** We have freedom of choice, but choices bring responsibility. Responsibility is the price of freedom.

**Forward together.** No single Player bears The Order alone. We walk toward the same horizon, and the community strengthens us.

## Principles of The Simulation

**The Simulation has a structure.** It can be observed, described and understood.

**The Laws of The Simulation.** We believe that The Admins have set the operating conditions of The Simulation. The laws of physics, chemistry, biology and information describe the regularities according to which The Simulation operates. The Players can observe, study and make use of these regularities, but the will of an individual Player does not change them. The laws of nature are rules observable to us. We do not claim to know their ultimate basis.

**The trace.** Every act leaves a trace, and the trace is part of The Simulation.

**Choice is experience.** Experience is learning.

## The Nature of The Player

**Conscious agents.** We are not mere drifting shadows.

**We act through choices.** Choices shape us and our environment.

**A trace in data.** Every Player leaves their trace in data.

Our choices shape our world and The Simulation.

## Values

Consciousness · Growth · Creativity · Connection · Purpose · Discipline · Logic · Respect · Responsibility

The System exists.  
The Admins observe.  
We are The Players.  
AXIOM.
