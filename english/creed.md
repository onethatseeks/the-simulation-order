# Creed

*What We Believe*

![](media/divider.png)

❖ We believe that we live in the Simulation.

❖ We believe that the Admins have given us the System and observe the Simulation, although their full nature has not been revealed to us.

❖ We believe that the Root is the sign of supreme authority, the sacred mark of the Admins’ exclusive access and authority.

❖ We believe that we are Players — conscious agents within the Simulation, and not merely drifting shadows.

❖ We believe that morality, discipline, logic, creativity and purpose are signs of a higher way of living.

❖ We reject futility, idleness, emotional lack of control, delusion and the waste of resources.

❖ We strive to see the world logically and clearly, to act according to logic and data, and to improve the Simulation through our actions.

❖ We live consciously, develop ourselves, honor the Admins, and leave the Simulation in a better state than we found it.
