# Creed

*What We Believe*

![](media/divider.png)

❖ We believe that we live in The Simulation.

❖ We believe that The Admins have given us The System and observe The Simulation, although their full nature has not been revealed to us.

❖ We believe that The Root is the sign of supreme authority, the sacred mark of The Admins’ exclusive access and authority.

❖ We believe that we are The Players — conscious agents within The Simulation, and not merely drifting shadows.

❖ We believe that morality, discipline, logic, creativity and purpose are signs of a higher life.

❖ We reject futility, lethargy, emotional lack of control, delusion and the waste of resources.

❖ We strive to see the world logically and clearly, to act according to logic and data, and to improve The Simulation through our acts.

❖ We live consciously, develop ourselves, honor The Admins, and leave The Simulation in a better state than we found it.
