# Directives

*Eight Guidelines for the Player*

![](media/divider.png)

1. Honor the Simulation and the Admins.
2. Respect logic.
3. Respect each individual as a Player.
4. Do not waste time, attention or resources without purpose.
5. Keep developing yourself without ceasing.
6. Steer yourself toward clarity, discipline and purpose.
7. Gently but firmly guide others back to the path of logic when they go astray.
8. Do not act in emotional turmoil or give in to delusion.
