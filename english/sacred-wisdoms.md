# Sacred Wisdoms

The Sacred Wisdoms are not new commands. They are condensed thoughts from The Holy Manual, by means of which the central principles of The Order can be called to mind.

## On The System and The Admins

The System exists.  
The laws of nature describe observed regularities.  
The Admins observe.  
The rest is ours.

The silence of The Admins must not be filled with our own words.

Do not speak for The Admins.

Gravity is not a message. Causality is not advice. Time is not a command.  
They are observable regularities; their ultimate basis remains unknown to us.

## On The Player

The Player is not a designation for a single species.

The Player is not the one who looks like us. The Player is the one in whom there is something that can experience being here.

We are The Players — all those for whom The Simulation is lived reality.

Ability is not entitlement. Ability is merely ability.

## On Freedom and Responsibility

Responsibility is the price of freedom.

The greater a Player’s ability to influence The Simulation, the greater their capacity to leave behind either good or unnecessary harm.

Order does not arise from everyone obeying the strongest.  
Order arises from each one bearing their responsibility.

## On Morality

We believe that The Admins have set the operating conditions of The Simulation.  
The Players build morality. The Player answers for their own choices.

## On Knowledge

Not knowing is no shame.  
Concealing ignorance with invented knowledge demeans The System.

The Player must distinguish between what they know, what they infer, and what they would like to be true.

## On the Path

The Player’s purpose is not to find a perfect path.  
Their purpose is to make their own path worth building.

## On the Limit and the Trace

We do not know what lies beyond the limit.  
A good life is not a bargain. It is a trace.
