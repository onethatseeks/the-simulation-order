## Through the Eyes of an Era

Every era looks at the world through its own knowledge, culture and technical ability.

Human beings never examine reality in a complete vacuum. What we consider possible, impossible, understandable or incomprehensible also depends on what we know and what tools we have at our disposal.

The worldview of ancient people arose from their observations and the concepts of their time. People of later ages built their own models on new observations, measuring instruments and knowledge. Today, we are doing exactly the same.

This is my view of the world and the universe in the 2020s. It does not claim to be the final truth, but rather the interpretation of reality by a human being of this era — relying on the tools, concepts and knowledge we have now.

Perhaps a person of the future will look at our worldview the way we look at the ideas of past ages. Perhaps they will see something we are not yet able to see.
