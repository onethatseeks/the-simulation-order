## Key Principles

### The Principle of the Silence of The Admins

The Admins do not give The Players new messages, commands or personal instructions. We believe that the reality given by The Admins is known from the structure of The System and its laws. The Player’s own thoughts, moral preferences or goals must not be presented as the will of The Admins.

### The Distinction Between the Given and the Agreed

The Given Law is what The Players encounter regardless of their opinion: physics, time, causality and the other fundamental limits of The Simulation. The Agreed Law is the structure built by The Players: morality, agreements, social rules and other shared arrangements.

### The Definition of The Player

A conscious agent acting within The Simulation.

### The Principle of Impact

The Player must assess the consequences of their actions in light of how broadly those actions affect other Players and The Simulation. The wider the impact, the greater the reason to act with deliberation.

### The Principle of Not Knowing

One may form hypotheses about the unknown, but a hypothesis must not be disguised as knowledge. The Player must be able to say “I do not know” when there is no knowledge.

## Sacred Symbols

**The Root [•]** — The sacred sign of The Admins’ supreme authority, of their access, and of the limited position of The Player.

**AXIOM** — A sacred word, a sign of conclusion, and a symbol of the foundation.

**The Horizon** — A shared direction, without the assumption of one single route.

## Key Pairs of Opposites

**Given / Agreed** — The regularities The Players encounter / the rules The Players build.

**Knowledge / Assumption** — What we can justifiably know / what we decide or imagine.

**Ability / Entitlement** — What we can do / what we ought to do.

**Freedom / Responsibility** — The possibility to choose / the duty to bear the consequences of the choice.

**Path / Horizon** — An individual way of walking / a shared direction.
