# Maintainers

This file identifies the people responsible for maintaining the canonical form of *The Simulation Order* and records the status and responsibility of its language editions.

## Canonical maintenance

| Role | Maintainer | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical maintainer | `onethatseeks` | Maintains the canonical work and approves or rejects proposed changes to its fundamental texts and core ideas. |

Changes to the canonical work require a compelling reason and the explicit approval of every person listed as a canonical maintainer.

## Language editions

| Language | Edition | Status | Responsible person(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finnish | Original Finnish edition | Original source | `onethatseeks` |
| English | English edition | Translation / international working edition | `onethatseeks` |

The Finnish edition is the original source of the work. Translations must identify the Finnish release on which they are based.

Responsibility for translating, editing or maintaining a language edition does not automatically grant authority to change the canonical work.

## Updating this file

Changes to language-edition responsibilities may be recorded as the project develops. Changes to canonical-maintainer authority must be explicit and documented.
