# The Simulation Order

*A 21st-century interpretation of reality.*

## Through the Eyes of an Era

Every era looks at the world through the knowledge, culture, and technological capabilities of its time.

The way we understand reality is never completely separated from the tools we have available to observe it, the concepts we have developed to describe it, and the limits of what we are currently capable of knowing.

This project begins from that perspective.

This is my view of the world and the universe in the 2020s. It does not claim to be the final truth, but rather an interpretation of reality by a human being of this era — based on the tools, concepts, and knowledge available to us now.

Perhaps people in the future will look at our worldview in the same way we look at the ideas of past eras. Perhaps they will see something we are not yet capable of seeing.

I have had multiple Players helping me shape this. They will be credited in a separate Markdown file later.

## What Is The Simulation Order?

The Simulation Order is a philosophical framework built around a simple possibility:

> **What if reality is a system?**

It presents a particular view of reality, consciousness, responsibility, and existence, as expressed in this work.

It explores questions concerning:

- the nature of reality
- consciousness and existence
- the limits of human knowledge
- the possibility of an underlying System
- the possibility of intelligence or agency beyond our current understanding
- human responsibility within the world we inhabit
- the relationship between knowledge, interpretation, and belief

The Simulation Order does not claim that the Simulation Hypothesis has been scientifically proven.

It is an exploration.

It is a philosophical proposition — something to examine, question, challenge, and interpret.

## The Holy Manual

The central text of The Simulation Order is **The Holy Manual** (*Pyhä Ohjekirja*).

It introduces the fundamental concepts of the worldview:

- the Simulation
- the System
- the Root
- the Admins
- the Players
- the Given Law
- the Agreed Law
- AXIOM

The Holy Manual is not intended to provide answers to questions that cannot currently be answered.

The silence of the Admins must not be filled with our own words.

## Sacred Wisdoms

The **Sacred Wisdoms** (*Pyhät Viisaudet*) collect principles and observations that form the ethical and philosophical core of the work.

Among them:

> **Ability is not entitlement. Ability is merely ability.**

> **The silence of the Admins must not be filled with our own words.**

> **We believe the Admins have given us the System. They have not given us all its answers.**

---

## Science and Philosophy

The Simulation Order does not reject science.

Science remains our strongest method for investigating the observable universe.

This project instead attempts to distinguish between different levels of certainty:

- observation
- scientific theory
- philosophical interpretation
- speculation
- belief

The Simulation Hypothesis belongs, at present, primarily to the philosophical and speculative domain rather than established scientific fact.

The distinction matters.

A question does not become a fact simply because it is interesting.

## A Defined Work

The Simulation Order is a defined philosophical work.

It presents a particular view of reality, consciousness, responsibility, and existence as understood by its authors. It is offered as a complete expression of that view, not as an open document to be continuously altered by outside interpretation or changing opinion.

The work may be questioned, criticized, discussed, interpreted, and challenged.

Its ideas may be rejected.

But disagreement does not change the work itself.

Anyone is free to build upon these ideas, reinterpret them, or create a different version of them. Such developments should exist as their own works or forks rather than silently changing the original.

The canonical version of The Simulation Order should remain stable.

Changes to its fundamental texts or core ideas should only be made when there is a compelling reason to do so and when the change has been explicitly accepted by all maintainers of the canonical work.

Such changes should be exceptional, deliberate, and documented in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

The world may change.

Human knowledge may change.

Our understanding of the universe may change.

But this work remains a record of the worldview we chose to express.

If someone sees the world differently, they are free to build their own.

> **The Simulation Order may be questioned by anyone.  
> It may be changed only by those entrusted with its canonical form.**

Read it as a proposition.

Question it.

Interpret it.
